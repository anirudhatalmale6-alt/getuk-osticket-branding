How can we help?

Log a support request with the GetUK team and track it through to resolution. Every request raised here gets a ticket reference, so you and we are always looking at the same history.

Raising a ticket
Use Open a New Ticket below. Tell us what you were doing, what happened, and roughly when it started — that is usually enough for us to get moving without a round of questions first.

Checking on something already raised
Use Check Ticket Status with your ticket number and the email address you raised it from.

If something is down right now
For anything urgent, call the team on 01457 337 247 rather than waiting on a ticket reply.
